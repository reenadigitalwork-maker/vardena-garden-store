01 / PLAN YOUR SPACE

First, understand your sunlight.

Notice which areas receive direct sun and at what time of day. Also look for strong wind, reflected heat and areas that stay shaded. Compare those conditions with the light requirements of the particular plant you choose.

Tip: observe your space at different times rather than judging sunlight from a single visit.

02 / CHOOSE YOUR CONTAINER

A good pot needs more than good looks.

Check the pot's size, weight and drainage before planting. For most common container plants, drainage holes help excess water escape. Choose a growing medium suited to the plant rather than assuming every plant needs the same mix.

On balconies, consider the filled pot's weight and place containers where they can be reached and watered safely.

03 / BUILD A CARE ROUTINE

Water according to what you observe.

Don't assume every pot needs water on the same schedule. Check the growing medium and learn the needs of each plant. When watering, allow excess water to drain and avoid leaving pots sitting in standing water unless specific care guidance calls for it.

Heat, wind, pot size and plant type can all change watering needs.
