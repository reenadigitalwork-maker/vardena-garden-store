YOUR GROWING NOTES

You don't need a garden. You need a beginning.

Start with the light you have, choose plants that suit your routine, and build one small habit at a time. A balcony, a windowsill or a sunny shelf can be enough.

THE NO-PRESSURE STARTER GUIDE

Start small. Learn as you grow.

Three practical checks before you bring a new plant home.

01 / ☼

Notice your light

Observe how much direct and indirect sunlight reaches your balcony or windowsill throughout the day.

02 / ◌

Choose for your routine

Be honest about your time. A low-maintenance plant can be a better first step than a demanding one.

03 / ⌁

Make drainage a priority

For many potted plants, drainage holes help excess water escape and reduce waterlogged roots.
